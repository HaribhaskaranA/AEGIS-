AEGIS SAR // SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & MULTI-AGENT PRINCIPLES

1. Decentralized Multi-Agent Search Principles

Rather than relying on a single centralized ground control station that can fail in rugged mountains or degraded RF environments, AEGIS uses decentralized information-gain path planning. Each drone maintains an onboard probability belief map of the 25 km² disaster grid and plans trajectories along maximum entropy gradients.

2. Ad-Hoc RF Mesh Gossip Consensus

Drones communicate using localized peer-to-peer gossip packets over a 915 MHz RF mesh link (effective radio line-of-sight ≤ 1,200m). When two drones fly within communication range, they fuse their belief maps. In areas separated by mountain ridges, relay drones bridge the partitioned clusters.

3. Dynamic Voronoi Partitioning & Failover

If a drone runs out of battery, experiences motor loss, or is disabled by mid-air turbulence, neighboring drones automatically detect the missing heartbeat within 12 seconds and dynamically adjust their search boundaries using weighted Voronoi tessellation to guarantee zero coverage gaps.

4. Ethical Human-in-the-Loop Casualty Verification

In accordance with international search and rescue best practices, automated thermal and optical sensor detections are treated strictly as probabilistic hypotheses. Ground rescue and medevac units are never dispatched without explicit human operator confirmation.
